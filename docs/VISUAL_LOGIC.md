# Visual Logic

- **Camera and survey instrument**: immediately establish observation versus physical reference.
- **Red candidate boundary**: indicates a visible but unresolved claim.
- **Teal reference boundary and points**: indicate validated or independently supported evidence.
- **Amber rays and crosshairs**: indicate observation, attention, and transfer—not truth.
- **Identity triptych**: research is shown with field instruments, industry with pipe stock, and community with a connected human network. The central Joo Yong-Hoe block explains why the three environments belong to one platform.
- **No fabricated portrait**: the public package avoids inventing a likeness. A real portrait or field photograph can replace the identity illustration later.
