# JoosAtlas v7 — Design system

## Direction

**Scientific Editorial × Industrial Documentary**

The visual system combines:
- restrained product storytelling and spatial pacing
- scientific editorial hierarchy
- mission- and evidence-oriented data presentation
- cross-disciplinary research identity
- field-documentary photography and captions

## Colour roles

- Deep navy — field depth, technical systems, audit sections
- Warm ivory — paper, evidence, theory and biography
- Brass — section index, observation and attention
- Mineral teal — validated evidence and metric continuity
- Cobalt — the physical primitive visible in the field photographs
- Muted red — discrepancy, refusal and claim limits

## Type roles

- Serif — research question, interpretation and narrative authority
- Sans serif — explanatory prose and navigation
- Monospace — coordinates, labels, release status and metric metadata

## Image roles

- Total-station imagery — research and physical datum
- Pipe-yard crop — Daejin Pipe as an operating company
- Welding image — Joo Yong-Hoe as a hands-on industrial operator
- ACERS identity — professional and alumni community
- TS plots — selected object-space evidence, never decorative charts

## Page architecture

1. `index.html` — JoosAtlas platform and public identity
2. `case-001.html` — theory, field realization, interactive evidence and claim boundary
3. `about.html` — Joo Yong-Hoe across research, industry and community
